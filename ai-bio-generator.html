Write a Clear Bio for LinkedIn, X, Upwork and Instagram

Your bio is often the first thing a client, recruiter or follower reads. It should say who you are, what you do and how to get in touch, in the space each platform allows. This generator turns your role and skills into ready-to-edit bios for each platform and checks the character limits for you. It uses only the details you type, so nothing is made up about your experience.

💼 LinkedIn & Upwork

LinkedIn: two headline options (220-character limit) plus an About section. Upwork: a short overview you can build on, plus a 160-character X bio.

📱 X & Instagram

Short bios that fit 160 characters (X) and 150 characters (Instagram), with the character count shown for each version.

How to Use the Bio Generator

  1. Type your role first, then your main skills separated by commas. You can add experience too, for example: Web Developer, React, Node.js, 5 years.
  2. Choose a platform style: LinkedIn, X / Upwork or Instagram. The result updates when you change the style.
  3. Click Generate Bios (or press Enter), then Copy Bios and paste the text into your profile.
  4. Edit the result: add one or two real results, projects or clients so your bio stands out.

What a Strong Bio Includes

  • Who you are: your role or niche in the first few words, such as Web Developer rather than a vague title.
  • What you work with: three to five skills, tools or services that clients search for.
  • Proof: real numbers, projects or years of experience. Only claim badges, rankings or results you can show.
  • A next step: a short invitation to connect, message you or view your portfolio.

Privacy

Your bio is generated by JavaScript in your browser. What you type is not sent to MyToolX servers and is not stored. This page loads Tailwind CSS and Google AdSense from third parties, which may set cookies as described in our Privacy Policy.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does the Bio Generator work?

You type your role, optionally followed by skills and years of experience separated by commas, and choose LinkedIn, X / Upwork or Instagram. The tool arranges your details into bios that fit each platform's limits using built-in rules in your browser, not an AI model.

Is this tool free?

Yes. It is free with no registration or subscription, and you can generate as many bios as you like.

What platforms does it cover?

LinkedIn (headline and About section), X and Upwork (a short X bio and an Upwork overview) and Instagram. The character limits used are 220 for a LinkedIn headline, 160 for an X bio and 150 for an Instagram bio. Platforms can change their limits, so check the app before publishing.

Is my information saved anywhere?

No. The text you type is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers or stored. The page itself loads Tailwind CSS and Google AdSense from third parties.

Why is my bio so short?

The generator only uses what you provide. Add skills and years of experience separated by commas for a fuller result, then add your own projects and results.

How long should an Upwork or LinkedIn bio be?

Keep a LinkedIn headline under 220 characters. For the About section and Upwork overview, a few short paragraphs or bullets that cover who you are, what you do and how to contact you usually work well. Add specific results so it reads as a real profile.
