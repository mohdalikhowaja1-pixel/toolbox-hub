How to Plan an Article Outline That Works

An outline is the skeleton of an article: one H1 title, H2 headings for the main sections and H3 headings for the details inside each section. Writing it first keeps you on topic, makes sure you answer the reader's real question, and saves editing time later. This generator reads your topic, recognises common article types (how-to guides, comparisons, "best of" lists, reviews, explainers and general guides) and builds a matching H1/H2/H3 structure with notes on what to write under each heading.

📐 Structure that matches your topic

Type "How to start a podcast" and you get steps and troubleshooting. Type "WordPress vs Wix" and you get a comparison table and a "which to choose" section.

✍️ Notes, not filler

Each section tells you what to cover, where to add your own examples and sources, and what to verify. It never invents facts, prices or statistics about your topic.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Article Outline Generator work?

You enter a topic. The tool recognises the intent (how-to, comparison, list, etc.) and builds a matching H1, alternative titles, and structured headings.

Does it write the article for me?

No. It creates the structure and tells you what to cover under each heading. You add the facts, examples and sources, so the final article is original and accurate.
