How to Plan an Article Outline That Works

An outline is the skeleton of an article: one H1 title, H2 headings for the main sections and H3 headings for the details inside each section. Writing it first keeps you on topic, makes sure you answer the reader's real question, and saves editing time later. This generator reads your topic, recognises common article types (how-to guides, comparisons, "best of" lists, reviews, explainers and general guides) and builds a matching H1/H2/H3 structure with notes on what to write under each heading.

📐 Structure that matches your topic

Type "How to start a podcast" and you get steps and troubleshooting. Type "WordPress vs Wix" and you get a comparison table and a "which to choose" section.

✍️ Notes, not filler

Each section tells you what to cover, where to add your own examples and sources, and what to verify. It never invents facts, prices or statistics about your topic.

How to Use the Article Outline Generator

  1. Type your topic or focus keyword in the box. A full phrase works best, for example how to start a freelance career or best laptops for students.
  2. Click Generate Outline (or press Enter). The outline appears in the dark panel with three alternative titles and a short SEO checklist.
  3. Click Copy Outline and paste it into your editor. Replace the notes with your own research, examples and sources.
  4. Change the topic and generate again to try a different angle. Use Clear to start over.

What the Headings Mean

  • H1: the title of the page. Use one H1 and include your focus keyword where it reads naturally.
  • H2: the main sections, usually the questions a reader wants answered.
  • H3: sub-points inside an H2, such as individual steps, pros and cons or examples. Do not skip heading levels.

Tips for Turning an Outline into a Strong Article

  • Answer the main question near the top, then explain the details.
  • Add your own experience, real examples and sources. Outlines help with structure, but original information is what makes an article useful.
  • Check prices, dates and product details on official sites before you publish them.
  • Write for readers first. Headings should describe the section clearly, not just repeat a keyword.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does the Article Outline Generator work?

You enter a topic. The tool recognises whether it is a how-to, comparison, "best" list, review, question or general topic and builds an H1, alternative titles, H2/H3 headings and writing notes to match. It uses built-in rules in your browser, not an AI model.

Does it write the article for me?

No. It creates the structure and tells you what to cover under each heading. You add the facts, examples and sources, so the final article is original and accurate.

Is this tool free and private?

Yes. It is free with no account. The topic you type is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers or stored. The page itself loads Tailwind CSS and Google AdSense from third parties.

Which topics work best?

Specific phrases work best, such as "how to start a podcast", "Notion vs Obsidian", "best budget headphones" or "what is a CRM". Very short or vague topics produce a general guide structure.

Will a good outline help my article rank?

A clear heading structure makes an article easier to read and to understand for search engines, but it does not guarantee rankings. Original, accurate and helpful content matters most.

Can I use the outlines for any niche?

Yes. The structure applies to technology, business, health, education, finance and more. For topics where accuracy matters, such as health or money, rely on qualified sources and cite them.
