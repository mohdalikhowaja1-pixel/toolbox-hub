ℹ️ About MyToolX: Free Tools, Honest Pricing, Privacy First

MyToolX is a small, friendly project with one simple idea. Give people useful tools they can open in a browser and use right away, without making an account, without watching an ad wall, and without sending their data anywhere. Most of what you see here is free and always will be. A few premium templates and custom services also exist, so the lights can stay on and the free tools can keep improving.

🔒 What "Private" Actually Means Here

When you use one of our free tools, the work happens inside your own browser. Your text stays in your browser. Your image stays in your browser. Your passwords, your JSON, your CV, your calculations, all of it stays on your device. We cannot see any of it, because nothing is sent to a server. There is no account to create, no dashboard on our end, and no database collecting your files. The only thing we ever receive is anonymous page traffic through Google Analytics, so we know which tools people actually use and which ones need work.

🛍️ Premium Templates and How Payment Works

Some of our work is premium. Think of ATS ready resume packs, Excel toolkits for freelancers, contract templates, and small custom builds. These are optional extras, and they are priced fairly with no subscription traps. Today you can pay by direct bank transfer. Once our card processor approvals finish with Lemon Squeezy and Whop, card payments will open up worldwide as well, so you will be able to choose whichever method suits you. Either way, delivery is manual and personal. You pay, we confirm, you receive your files. No middleman holds your purchase. You can see what is available in the Premium Store.

💬 Talk to a Real Person

If something breaks, if a tool needs a feature, if you want a custom template built for your team, or if you just want to say hello, use our contact form. A real human reads every message and replies quickly. We are a small team, and that is on purpose. You are not talking to a support queue.

🌱 Why We Keep Going

The web has become loud. Pop ups, cookie banners, forced sign ups, ten step wizards just to compress a photo. We think it can be quieter than that. So we build tools that respect your time and your files, and we hope that if they help you, you will tell one other person about them. That is the whole business model.
