Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment, which includes both principal repayment and interest charges.

Can I calculate loan tenure in months instead of years?

Yes! Simply change the 'Tenure Type' dropdown to Months.
