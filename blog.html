1. Typing & Visuals

⌨️ How to Improve Your Typing Speed (and Why It Matters)

In the digital age, your keyboard is your primary tool. Whether you are a programmer, a writer, or a student, the speed at which you type directly impacts your daily productivity. The average person types at about 40 Words Per Minute (WPM). However, professionals often hit 60-80 WPM.

Focus on Accuracy First: It is tempting to rush, but hitting the wrong keys constantly will slow you down. If you make a mistake, force yourself to correct it. Muscle memory is built on repetition. Use our 3D Typing Speed Test to track your accuracy. Aim for 95% or higher before trying to go faster.

🖼️ Why Compressing Images is Crucial for Web Performance

If you run a website or an online store, page speed is everything. Google uses "Core Web Vitals" as a ranking factor, and one of the biggest culprits for slow websites is unoptimized images. A single photo from a modern smartphone can be 5MB or larger.

The Problem with Uploading: Most online compressors require you to upload your photos to their server, which raises privacy concerns. With our Image Compressor, your images are never uploaded. The tool uses HTML5 Canvas to process the image locally in your browser.

2. AI & Content Creation

🤖 How to Write Better AI Prompts

AI language models are only as good as the instructions you give them. If you type "write a blog post," you will get a generic result. To get professional output, you need a structured prompt.

A great prompt includes: Role, Task, Context, and Format. Instead of memorizing this, use our AI Prompt Studio. It asks you a series of questions and generates a perfectly structured prompt for ChatGPT, Gemini, or Midjourney.

✨ Writing a Professional Social Media Bio

Your bio is often the first thing a client or recruiter reads. It needs to instantly communicate who you are and what you do, while staying within strict character limits. Our AI Bio Generator analyzes your skills and generates highly professional bios for LinkedIn, X, and Instagram.

📑 Planning an Article Outline That Works

An outline is the skeleton of an article. Writing it first keeps you on topic and saves editing time later. Our Article Outline Generator reads your topic and builds a matching H1/H2/H3 structure, ensuring you never stare at a blank page again.

As a freelancer, the price you quote is not what you take home. Between Stripe and PayPal, your profit margin shrinks fast. Use our Fee Calculator to see exactly what a gateway will take. It also features a "gross-up" calculator to ensure you receive your exact target amount.

📈 Building Wealth with SIPs

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to compound wealth steadily. Our SIP Calculator projects the future value of your investments by applying monthly compounding, helping you understand how small investments grow over 10 or 20 years.
