💳 Payment Fee & Contract Earnings Suite

A multi-purpose financial suite designed for freelancers, agencies, and online businesses to calculate gateway transaction fees and estimate contract earnings.

Suite Features & Tab Breakdown:

  • 💰
    1. Payment Fee Calculator: Calculates total processing fees, net take-home amount, and required invoice total for Stripe and PayPal across USD, EUR, GBP, PKR, and INR currencies.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is 'Ask / Invoice Amount'?

It is the reverse fee calculation showing the total amount you must invoice so that after payment gateway deductions, you receive your exact net payout.

How are contract earnings calculated?

Calculations multiply hourly rate by weekly hours across 52 weeks in a year and 4.33 weeks per month for accurate projection.
