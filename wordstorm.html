How to Play WordStorm

WordStorm is a falling-words typing game. Phrases drift down from the top of the stage and you must clear them before they reach the ground. Every world is a different atmosphere, every phrase is a real English fragment, and every heart you lose cuts your run short. The faster and cleaner you type, the higher your multiplier climbs, and the more points each phrase is worth.

Step by step (desktop)

  1. Press Start. A 3-2-1 countdown plays, then the Ocean world appears and phrases start falling.
  2. Start typing any phrase. The moment your first letter matches a phrase, the game locks onto it. Backspace if you made a mistake.
  3. Finish the phrase. When you have typed every character, the phrase bursts and scores points. No Enter key needed.
  4. Watch the multiplier. Each cleared phrase raises your multiplier from 1.0x up to 3.0x. A phrase that reaches the ground resets it to 1.0x and costs a heart.
  5. Clear 10 phrases per world. Then the world changes: Ocean, Sky, Mountain, Space, Night City. Each world speeds up and uses longer phrases.
  6. Survive all five worlds with hearts remaining to earn the Legendary badge.

Step by step (mobile)

  1. Tap Mode activates automatically on phones and tablets.
  2. Tap any falling phrase to clear it. A quick tap is all it takes.
  3. Lives, multiplier, worlds and score all work exactly the same as Type Mode.
  4. Try WordStorm on a laptop or desktop for the full typing experience with 2x scoring potential.

Worlds, Hearts, Multipliers and Your Personal Leaderboard

Every run is a climb through five themed worlds. Each world has its own atmosphere, its own phrases, and its own speed. Your score, your best multiplier, and the highest world you have ever reached are saved in your browser so a strong run is never lost.

🌊 Five worlds

Ocean, Sky, Mountain, Space, Night City. Each world changes the background, phrase length, and falling speed.

❤️ Three hearts

A phrase that reaches the ground costs one heart. Lose all three and the run ends. Clear a world without losing a heart for a +50 bonus.

🔥 Combo multiplier

Every cleared phrase raises your multiplier by 0.1x, up to 3.0x. A miss resets it to 1.0x. Score per phrase = length × multiplier.

⭐ Star rating

Three stars for reaching Space or beyond with 80%+ accuracy. Two stars for clearing three worlds. One star for finishing a run.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play WordStorm?

Phrases fall from the top of the screen. On desktop, start typing any phrase and the game locks onto it. On mobile, tap any falling phrase. Clear enough phrases to advance to the next world. A phrase that reaches the ground costs you a heart.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. On touch devices WordStorm automatically switches to Tap Mode. Both modes share the same lives, combos, worlds, and scores. Desktop typing gives more points because keyboard play is faster, so the highest scores come from a laptop or desktop.

How many worlds are there?

Five: Ocean, Sky, Mountain, Space, Night City. Each world has its own background, phrases, and speed. You clear 10 phrases per world before the next one appears.

Are the phrases random or repeated?

Every phrase is picked fresh from a themed word pool with an anti-repeat buffer, so you never type the same phrase twice in a row within a session. Each world uses its own vocabulary so the experience feels distinct.

Does the game save my high score?

Yes. Your best score, best multiplier, and highest world are saved locally in your browser. Nothing is ever sent to a server.
