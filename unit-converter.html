Standard Mathematical Formulas Used

Our client-side conversion engine relies on standardized international scientific ratios:

  • Kilometers to Miles: 1 km ≈ 0.621371 mi
  • Meters to Feet: 1 m ≈ 3.28084 ft
  • Inches to Centimeters: 1 in = 2.54 cm
  • Kilograms to Pounds: 1 kg ≈ 2.20462 lbs
  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Client-Side Speed & Privacy

Unlike web apps that perform server-side calculations via API calls, all conversion algorithms run locally using JavaScript in your browser memory. This guarantees zero latency, instant real-time updates as you type, and 100% data privacy with zero server logging.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What unit categories are supported by this converter?

It supports Length & Distance (km, miles, meters, feet, inches, cm), Weight & Mass (kg, lbs), and Temperature (°C, °F).

Are conversions calculated live without clicking a submit button?

Yes. As soon as you type any numeric value or change the unit selection mode, the converted output calculates instantly in real time.

Is this tool free and private?

Yes, 100% free with no registration required. All mathematical calculations run locally inside your browser, meaning no input data is ever sent to external servers.

How accurate are the conversion outputs?

Our conversion engine utilizes standard 64-bit floating-point precision ratios, providing high accuracy suitable for academic, technical, and commercial applications.

Why do different countries use Imperial vs Metric systems?

The Metric system is used globally due to its base-10 decimal simplicity, whereas the Imperial system remains standard in the United States, Myanmar, and Liberia due to historical regulatory frameworks.
