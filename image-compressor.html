Compress Images Without Uploading Them

Most online image compressors require you to upload your files to their servers, wait for processing, and then download the result. This tool works differently. It uses your browser's built-in HTML5 Canvas to compress and resize images entirely on your device. This means it is faster, works offline, and guarantees 100% privacy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All image processing happens locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your files never leave your device.

What image formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG, and WebP images. The output will be in JPG format to ensure the best compression.
