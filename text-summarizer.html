How Our Local NLP Algorithm Works

You might wonder how a free tool can summarize text without a live connection to ChatGPT. We use a sophisticated client-side algorithm based on Natural Language Processing (NLP).

  • Sentence Scoring: The tool reads every sentence and assigns it a score based on the frequency of key terms (ignoring common words like "the" and "and").
  • Signal Boosting: Sentences that contain transition words (like "Therefore," "Conclusion," "However") or critical warnings are given a higher priority to ensure the core message is preserved.
  • Context Locking: To prevent the summary from feeling like disconnected bullet points, the algorithm always anchors the summary with the first and last sentences of your text, ensuring the original introduction and conclusion are maintained.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. We built this tool with a privacy-first architecture. All text processing happens locally inside your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never transmitted to MyToolX or any third-party AI provider.

How long should the original text be?

For the best results, we recommend pasting text that is at least 100 words. If the text is too short, the summarizer will simply return the original text, as there isn't enough content to extract a meaningful summary.

Will the summary sound like a robot?

Because this tool extracts the most important sentences from your original text, the summary will sound exactly like the original author. It does not "rewrite" the text like a paid AI chatbot might. It pulls the best sentences to give you a quick overview.

What is the difference between the summary targets?

Short Overview: Great for getting the absolute main points of a long article. Balanced Summary: Retains more context and examples. Detailed Summary: Nearly half the length of the original text, best for complex topics where you want to keep the majority of the details.
