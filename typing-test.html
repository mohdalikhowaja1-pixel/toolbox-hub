Frequently Asked Questions

How is WPM calculated?

WPM (Words Per Minute) is calculated by taking the total characters typed correctly, dividing by 5 (the standard word length), and dividing by the time elapsed in minutes.

What is a good typing speed?

The average typing speed is around 40 WPM. A speed of 60-70 WPM is considered professional, and anything above 80 WPM is highly advanced.

Does this tool track my keystrokes?

No. All typing tests and calculations happen entirely within your browser using JavaScript. We do not track, store, or send your keystrokes to any server.
