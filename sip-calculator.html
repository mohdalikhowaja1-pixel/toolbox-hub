📈 Mutual Fund SIP & Wealth Investment Calculator

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to compound wealth steadily by investing fixed monthly amounts along with optional initial advance lump-sum deposits.

Key Features & Metrics:

  • 💵
    1. Out-of-Pocket Invested: Calculates the exact total cash deposited through your initial deposit plus recurring monthly SIP contributions.
  • 📈
    2. Estimated Wealth Gain: Measures compounding interest returns generated over your selected investment time horizon.
  • 🏆
    3. Future Maturity Corpus: Shows the net total value of your investment corpus at the end of the investment duration.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the advantage of adding an initial advance deposit?

An initial lump-sum deposit compound grows alongside your monthly SIP deposits, accelerating total returns.

How is compound growth calculated?

It applies standard monthly compound formulas to recurring deposits combined with annual compound interest on initial advance amounts.
