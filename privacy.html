🛡️ Privacy Policy

At MyToolX (mytoolx.org), accessible from https://mytoolx.org, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. Most of our tools process data entirely within your browser (client-side), meaning your text, files, and inputs are never transmitted or stored on any server.

Cookies and Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their interaction with our platform.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

Log Files & Web Analytics

MyToolX follows standard procedures of using log files provided by web hosting services (such as GitHub Pages). The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, and referring/exit pages. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, users have the right to request access to, correction of, or erasure of any personal data collected. Since MyToolX does not require user accounts or store personal user inputs, we do not retain identifiable personal database records.

Contact Us

If you have questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, feel free to submit an inquiry through our Contact Form.
