    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is my text stored or uploaded anywhere?

    MyToolX does not store your text. For the online check, the text is transmitted securely to the LanguageTool API and, if you explicitly enable the AI option, to a free AI model provider for processing. If you need complete privacy, avoid pasting sensitive content.

    Is there a word limit for checking?

    Yes. Input is limited to 300 words per check to keep results fast and stay within the free service limits.
