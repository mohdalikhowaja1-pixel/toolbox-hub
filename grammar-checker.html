    Free Online Grammar, Spelling & Punctuation Proofreading

    Writing clear and error-free content is essential for professional communication, academic submissions, email correspondence, and online publishing. The MyToolX Grammar & Spelling Checker uses the open-source LanguageTool engine to detect spelling mistakes, grammar errors, missing commas, wrong capitalization and spacing problems in more than 30 languages, then applies the corrections for you in one click.

    ⚡ Automatic Typo & Grammar Fixes

    Corrects misspellings, wrong word forms, informal shorthand such as "thx" and "abt", and repeated words.

    🔤 Capitalization & Punctuation

    Capitalizes sentences and the pronoun "I", fixes spacing around commas and periods, and adds missing end punctuation.

    🌍 Works Worldwide

    Choose your language or let the tool detect it automatically, including English, Spanish, French, German, Arabic and more.

    🛟 Offline Fallback

    If the online service is busy, built-in browser rules still fix common English mistakes.

    How Your Text Is Handled

    To provide accurate grammar checking, your text is sent securely over HTTPS to the LanguageTool API and, when the AI option is on, to a free AI model provider, and returned with suggested fixes. MyToolX does not save your text on its own servers. Avoid pasting passwords, banking details, or other highly sensitive information. See our Privacy Policy for details.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

    How does this Grammar Checker work?

    Your text is checked by the free LanguageTool engine for grammar, spelling and punctuation. We then apply the best suggestion for each issue and run an extra clean-up for spacing, capitalization and end punctuation in your browser.

    Is my text stored or uploaded anywhere?

    MyToolX does not store your text. For the online check, the text is transmitted securely to the LanguageTool API and, when the AI option is on, to a free AI model provider for processing. If you need complete privacy, avoid pasting sensitive content.

    Is there a word limit for checking?

    Yes. Input is limited to 300 words per check to keep results fast and stay within the free service limits.

    Can I use this for non-English languages?

    Yes. Select your language or use auto-detect. More than 30 languages are supported. Extra shorthand fixes (like "thx" to "thanks") apply to English only.

    Why didn't it fix everything?

    No automatic checker is perfect. Always read the result before using it, especially for names, technical terms and creative writing.
