Instant Client-Side Grammar & Proofreading

Writing clear and error-free content is essential for professional communication, academic submissions, email correspondence, and online publishing. The MyToolX Grammar & Spelling Checker offers a lightweight, client-side proofreading solution that runs directly in your browser without transmitting your private drafts to remote cloud servers.

Why Use Client-Side Grammar Correction?

Most cloud-based grammar checkers send every word you type to third-party APIs or servers. If you are handling confidential documents, proprietary code notes, or personal correspondence, uploading text online carries privacy risks. Our tool operates using Javascript in your browser, keeping your text completely offline and private.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does this Grammar Checker work?

The tool utilizes a local dictionary mapping and pattern-matching rules to catch common spelling mistakes, informal slang (e.g., 'abt' to 'about'), duplicated words, and basic sentence capitalization errors.

Is my text stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. All proofreading operations happen locally inside your web browser. Nothing is sent to external servers, database logs, or third-party APIs.

Is there a word limit for checking?

Yes, to maintain real-time browser performance without freezing, the input is currently capped at 300 words per session.

Can I use this for non-English languages?

Currently, our client-side rule engine is optimized specifically for English spelling and basic grammar structures.
