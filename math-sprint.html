How to Play the Mental Math Sprint

The Mental Math Sprint is a fast, quiz-show style workout built on the math you meet every day: percentages on a discount tag, quick addition at the till, and simple financial sums. Each question gives you four options and one correct answer. After you press Start, a short 3-2-1 countdown gets you ready, then the clock begins. The goal is simple: answer as many as you can, correctly and quickly, before the timer runs out. The game adapts to your level, so it stays interesting whether you are warming up or chasing a personal best.

Step by step

  1. Choose your timer. Pick 30, 60 or 90 seconds from the dropdown. Your choice is saved for next time.
  2. Press Start. A 3-2-1 countdown appears, then the first question arrives and the clock starts ticking.
  3. Pick an option. Click or tap A, B, C, or D. The correct answer lights up green, and a wrong pick turns red while the right one glows green so you learn.
  4. Watch your tier climb. Correct answers and high accuracy unlock harder tiers, which bring new question types into play.
  5. Protect your streak. Every 7 correct answers in a row earns a milestone bonus. A miss resets the streak and costs one point, but never below zero.
  6. Beat your best. Your high score, best streak and best accuracy are saved in your own browser, so a strong run carries over to your next visit.

Difficulty Tiers, Streaks and Your Personal Leaderboard

There is no sign-up and no global ranking, because the best opponent you have is your own last score. Each session saves three numbers in your browser: your highest score, the best streak you have put together in one run, and your best accuracy. Your best accuracy also decides the tier you start at next time, so a strong player walks straight into harder questions.

🏅 Five difficulty tiers

Warmup, Standard, Pro, Elite and Master. Tiers unlock from your score and accuracy during a round, and higher tiers add reverse percentages, multi-step discounts, bill splitting, commission and tax.

🔥 Streaks and bonuses

Base +1 per correct answer, a +1 milestone at every 7-answer streak, plus a tier bonus that grows with difficulty. A wrong answer costs one point but never below zero.

📊 Saved on your device

Best score, best streak and best accuracy live in localStorage. Clearing your browser data will clear them, because nothing is ever sent to a server.

🎉 80% club

Finish a round with accuracy of 80% or higher and a celebration popup appears with confetti. Close it with the X to replay immediately, or share your score with friends.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a round of the Mental Math Sprint?

You choose 30, 60 or 90 seconds before the round starts. A short 3-2-1 countdown lets you get ready, then the clock begins with the first question. Your choice of timer is remembered for your next visit.

Are the questions random or fixed?

Every question is generated fresh by the game. It remembers your last 25 questions and never repeats one inside that window, so sessions always feel new. Higher tiers add brand-new question families, so veterans keep meeting questions they have not seen before.

Does the game get harder as I improve?

Yes. Five difficulty tiers unlock as your score and accuracy rise. Tier 4 and 5 add reverse percentages, multi-step discounts, bill splitting, commission and tax. Your best accuracy is saved, so a strong player starts at a higher tier next visit.

How are the four options generated?

Each question has one correct answer and three plausible wrong answers built from common mistakes, such as swapping the percentage for the base, forgetting the second discount, or mixing up the total with the per-person amount. Every option is a whole number and no two options are ever the same.

Does the game save my high score?

Yes. Your best score, best streak and best accuracy are saved locally in your browser with localStorage. They stay on your device and are never sent to a server.

Is there a leaderboard?

Your leaderboard is personal. Your best numbers are saved in your browser, and both the Share button and the Copy challenge link button let you send your result to friends with a ready-to-paste message.
