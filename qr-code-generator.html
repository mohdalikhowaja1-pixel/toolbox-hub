Best Practices for Creating High-Converting QR Codes

To ensure your QR codes scan reliably across various print mediums and mobile screens, follow these essential design and technical guidelines:

  • High Contrast Ratio: Always maintain a high contrast between the dark foreground modules and light background color for camera lens readability.
  • Keep URLs Short: Shorter target URLs produce lower density QR matrices, which scan faster from longer distances.
  • Test Print Sizes: For print materials like posters, flyers, or business cards, ensure the printed QR code is at least 2 cm x 2 cm (0.8 x 0.8 inches) in dimension.
