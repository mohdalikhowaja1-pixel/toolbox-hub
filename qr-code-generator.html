Frequently Asked Questions

Is this QR code generator completely free?

Yes, 100% free with unlimited scans, no hidden fees, no registration, and no expiration date on any created codes.

Do the generated QR codes ever expire?

No. These are static QR codes where the destination data or URL is embedded directly inside the code matrix. As long as your destination link or text remains active, the QR code will work forever.

Are my target URLs or QR inputs saved on any server?

No. The QR matrix rendering happens entirely client-side inside your browser via JavaScript. Nothing is sent to external servers or database logs.
