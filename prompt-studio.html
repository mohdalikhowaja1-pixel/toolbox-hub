What each tool does and how to use it

  • Prompt Builder builds a structured prompt for your AI app. Describe what you need, add optional details, generate, then paste the prompt into your AI app. It prepares instructions; it does not answer them. For Midjourney it adds --ar and --no parameters; for other tools it uses plain, natural-sounding English.
  • Social Post Generator drafts a LinkedIn post, an X post or thread, or an Instagram Reel caption from your topic, key points, audience, objective, tone and call to action. It only uses the facts you give it and never invents statistics or trends.
  • YouTube Metadata Optimizer drafts title options, a description, tags, hashtags, an opening hook, a pinned comment and a checklist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool give real AI answers?

No. The Prompt Studio prepares prompts for apps such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Midjourney or DALL·E.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

The text you type is processed in your browser by this page's code and is not sent to MyToolX servers or any AI service, and it is not saved.
