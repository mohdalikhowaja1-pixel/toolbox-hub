AI Prompt Studio & Content Tools

Three free tools that run in your browser: a prompt builder for AI apps, a social post generator and a YouTube metadata optimizer. They use built-in rules, not an AI model, so the results are drafts for you to review.

What each tool does and how to use it

  • Prompt Studio builds a structured prompt (role, task, context, requirements, output format and a final check) for the task type you choose: writing, programming, business, marketing, research, education, image generation or YouTube content. Describe what you need, add optional details, generate, then paste the prompt into your AI app. It prepares instructions; it does not answer them. For Midjourney it adds --ar and --no parameters; for other tools it uses plain wording.
  • Social Post Generator drafts a LinkedIn post, an X post or thread, or an Instagram Reel caption from your topic, key points, audience, objective, tone and call to action. It only uses the facts you give it and never invents statistics or trends. For X it keeps the call to action and removes hashtags first when a post is too long.
  • YouTube Metadata Optimizer drafts title options, a description, tags, hashtags, an opening hook, a pinned comment and a checklist. It does not check search volume, competition or rankings; confirm keywords in YouTube Studio or YouTube search suggestions. Chapters are created only from timestamps you enter.

Example: for a LinkedIn post, enter the topic "fast websites for small businesses", add two real benefits as key points, choose Educate, and keep or replace the default call to action. You get a ready-to-edit post plus alternative opening lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool give real AI answers?

No. The Prompt Studio prepares prompts for apps such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, GitHub Copilot, Midjourney or DALL·E. The social and YouTube tools use fixed rules and templates, not an AI model.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

The text you type is processed in your browser by this page's code and is not sent to MyToolX servers or any AI service, and it is not saved. The page itself loads Google AdSense, which may use cookies as described in our Privacy Policy.

Is it free? Do I need an account or API key?

It is free, and no account, subscription or API key is needed.

Which AI apps can use the prompts?

Text prompts work in chat assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, and in GitHub Copilot Chat. Image prompts are written for Midjourney or general image generators. Different models can give different results, and no outcome is guaranteed.

How does the tool shorten X posts?

It keeps your main message and call to action, then removes hashtags first, then the optional extra point, and finally trims wording at a word boundary. The count shown is for the exact text you copy and is approximate because X counts links and emoji in its own way.

Are the YouTube suggestions based on search data?

No. They come only from the topic, keywords and details you enter. The tool never reports search volume, competition or trends and cannot promise rankings or views.

Why do posts or metadata sometimes need editing?

The generators only use what you provide and never invent facts, results or testimonials. The more specific your key points are, the more specific the draft will be.
